Privacy notice

What we collect when you contact us, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Last updated {{ SITE.updated_human }}.

Who we are

ILVERA Studios is run by its founders, Evan Surratt and Justin Whitmore. For anything about your data, email {{ SITE.email }}.

What we collect

When you use our inquiry form or email us, we receive the details you choose to send: your name, business name, email address, website or channel, and what you tell us about your project, timeline and budget.

This site doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we add visitor statistics, we'll use a privacy-friendly tool that doesn't use cookies, and we'll update this notice before we do.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you and to discuss a possible project. The legal bases are taking steps you've asked for before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries. We don't sell your data or add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it

The founders, plus the services that deliver our email and, when you use the form, the service that passes your message to our inbox. Those providers process it only to deliver it to us.

How long we keep it

If your inquiry doesn't lead to a project, we delete it within 12 months. If it does, we keep what we need to run the project and meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you, to stop using it, or to send it to you in a portable format. Email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in France, that's the CNIL.

Videos, fonts and links

Videos and fonts on this site are served from this site, not from third parties. Videos with sound play only when you press play. Links to other sites, such as YouTube or Instagram, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and its date.
