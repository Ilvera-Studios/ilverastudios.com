About

A small studio that delivers finished work.

ILVERA Studios is an independent production company founded by Evan Surratt and Justin Whitmore. We produce video for businesses and develop original entertainment of our own.

What we believe

Results first

People hire us for videos that get watched and do their job, not for the tools we use to make them.

Craft at every step

Story, script, picture, sound and packaging. AI-assisted methods make us fast; judgment and taste make the work good.

Honest by default

We label AI-generated characters, keep our own projects separate from client work, and never promise views or sales.

The founders

Two founders with different strengths.

One brings two decades in hospitality and entertainment; the other builds the production system and runs the creative. Both are involved in the work.

Where we work

Remote, in English and French.

We work remotely with businesses of all sizes, starting with independent brands and small and medium-sized companies in the United States, the United Kingdom and the rest of Europe. We produce in English and French.

We're a new studio, and we'll tell you exactly what we have and haven't done. What you'll get is direct access to the people making your work, a clear written scope, and finished files on the agreed date.

The name

ILVERA, said "il-VAIR-uh".

The name comes from the French il verra, "one will see", and vera, truth. It's a reminder of what we're for: work you can see, and claims you can check.

In French it's said "il-vé-RA".

How we work

Six steps, every project.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We learn what you sell, who you sell to and what you want video to do. You leave with an honest recommendation.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    Scope, deliverables, schedule and price, agreed in writing before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    Strategy, scripts, the look and, where needed, presenters or characters. You approve before we produce.

  4. 04

    Production

    Animation, editing, sound, captions and packaging.

  5. 05

    Review

    You review each cut. Revision rounds are set out in the proposal.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Final files for every platform, or publishing on your channel. Recurring work starts its next cycle with what the numbers taught us.
