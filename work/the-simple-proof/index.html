How it's made

A production system, not a pile of videos.

167 videos in under seven weeks, more than 20 a week, from one person. AI speeds up research, drafting and backgrounds; a person checks every claim and records every word.

  1. Script

    AI helps with research and drafting. A person checks every claim, because a math video is either right or wrong.

  2. Record

    A real presenter records the narration, on camera or voice only. No synthetic voices.

  3. Animate

    Diagrams are rendered from code (Python and Manim), so every shape and number on screen is exact. AI-generated images add backgrounds and scenes.

  4. Assemble

    An in-house tool transcribes the narration, checks it against the script and times every scene to the spoken word.

  5. Package and publish

    Titles are written around a question a curious stranger would click on. Long-form videos are mastered in 4K.

What it doesn't show yet: subscribers

The channel gained 184 subscribers in the period, about one for every 600 views. It went for reach first. Turning viewers into subscribers, with series formats, end screens and playlists, is the channel's current focus. On client projects we agree the goal first: reach, subscribers, leads or sales.
