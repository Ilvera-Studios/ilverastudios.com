Sienna Torneu

Educational chess channel · Digital presenter

Chess lessons for players rated about 400 to 1500, with a host who has her own look and a dry sense of humor.

Every game is animated move by move on a board, and every line is checked with a chess engine. The first lesson, Paul Morphy's 1858 Opera House Game, is finished, and the channel is getting ready to launch.

Microdrama

Why we make originals

Original IP is how a studio earns its own voice.

Owning our stories lets us try formats before we offer them to clients, build characters audiences follow, and develop properties that can grow into channels, partnerships and products of their own.

When a brand asks us for a series or a digital presenter, they're hiring the same team, the same craft and the same standards you see here.

How we use AI: our characters are created with AI tools, and we label them that way wherever they appear. No ILVERA character is presented as a real person.
