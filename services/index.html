Process

How a project runs.

Nothing is produced until you've approved the plan, and nothing goes live without your sign-off.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We learn what you sell, who you sell to and what you want video to do. You leave with an honest recommendation.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    Scope, deliverables, schedule and price, agreed in writing before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    Strategy, scripts, the look and, where needed, presenters or characters. You approve before we produce.

  4. 04

    Production

    Animation, editing, sound, captions and packaging.

  5. 05

    Review

    You review each cut. Revision rounds are set out in the proposal.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Final files for every platform, or publishing on your channel. Recurring work starts its next cycle with what the numbers taught us.
